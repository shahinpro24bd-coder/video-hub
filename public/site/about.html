About Me

Dr. Ananta Kumar Bhakta

MBBS | BCS (Health) | MS Orthopedics (NITOR) | Fellowship in Endoscopic Spine Surgery

Orthopedic & Spine Surgeon

Dr. Ananta Kumar Bhakta is an Orthopedic & Spine Surgeon and Endoscopic Spine Surgeon. He serves as Assistant Professor in the Orthopedics Department.

He completed MBBS, joined BCS (Health) and obtained MS in Orthopedics from NITOR. He has completed AO Trauma and Spine Training (India), AO Advance Cadaver Training (India, Hongkong), Fellowship in Endoscopic Spine Surgery and Endoscopic Cadaver Training (India).

He sees patients at Popular Diagnostic Center, Jashim Uddin Mor, Sector 4, Uttara (3rd Building, Room 504) from 6:00 PM to 8:00 PM, at Hicare General Hospital, Lake Drive Road, Sector 7, Uttara (General Building, Room 201) from 8:00 PM to 10:00 PM — Friday & Saturday off — and at Ahmed Medical Center, opposite Narsingdi Sadar Hospital on Friday, 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM. For serial booking: 01830-995336.
